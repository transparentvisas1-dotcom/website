## PROCESS STEPS

### H2
Our 5-Step Visa Application Process

---

### Step 1

#### Label
STEP 01

#### H3
Free Consultation

#### Body
Everything begins with a conversation. Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or email, and we'll discuss your travel plans, destination, and purpose of visit. We'll explain the visa type you need, the documents required, the estimated timeline, and our fees — all upfront, with no obligation.

#### What You Get
- Honest eligibility assessment
- Clear explanation of the visa process for your destination
- Document checklist tailored to your case
- Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
- Answers to all your questions

---

### Step 2

#### Label
STEP 02

#### H3
Profile Assessment & Strategy

#### Body
Once you decide to proceed, we conduct a thorough assessment of your profile. We evaluate your passport, travel history, financial standing, employment or business status, and ties to your home country. Based on this assessment, we develop a personalised application strategy designed to maximise your approval chances.

#### What We Evaluate
- Passport validity and travel history
- Financial stability (bank statements, ITR, salary)
- Employment or business documentation
- Purpose of visit evidence
- Previous visa refusals (if any)
- Overall risk factors and how to mitigate them

---

### Step 3

#### Label
STEP 03

#### H3
Document Preparation

#### Body
This is where our expertise truly shines. We prepare, review, and compile every document your application needs — to the exact standard that embassies expect. We don't leave anything to chance.

#### What We Prepare
- Application form filling and verification
- Cover letter tailored to your specific case
- Financial document review and organisation
- Travel itinerary and accommodation plans
- Supporting letters (employer, sponsor, inviter)
- Photograph and biometric guidance
- Any additional documents specific to your visa type

---

### Step 4

#### Label
STEP 04

#### H3
Application Submission

#### Body
With your complete document package assembled, we handle the application submission process. This includes online form submission, appointment booking (if required), and ensuring all materials are formatted and submitted correctly.

#### What We Handle
- Online application form submission
- Visa appointment booking at the embassy or VFS centre
- Document compilation in the correct order
- Pre-submission quality check
- Application fee payment guidance
- Biometric appointment preparation

---

### Step 5

#### Label
STEP 05

#### H3
Tracking & Approval

#### Body
After submission, we actively track your application and keep you informed of its status. When your visa is approved, we also assist with post-approval needs — flight bookings, travel insurance, and any final travel preparations.

#### What Happens After Submission
- Regular application status updates
- Embassy correspondence handling (if needed)
- Interview preparation coaching (for USA and other countries requiring interviews)
- Notification when your visa is approved
- Post-approval travel support (flights, insurance, itinerary)
- Guidance for your trip and entry requirements

## TIMELINE SECTION

### H2
Typical Processing Timelines

| Visa Type | Preparation Time | Embassy Processing | Total Estimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tourist / Visit Visa | 2–3 days | 5–15 working days | 1–3 weeks |
| Student Visa | 3–5 days | 4–12 weeks | 5–13 weeks |
| Work Visa | 5–7 days | 4–12 weeks | 5–13 weeks |
| Business Visa | 2–3 days | 5–15 working days | 1–3 weeks |
| Visa Refusal Re-application | 5–10 days | 5–15 working days | 2–4 weeks |

*Timelines are estimates and vary by country, visa type, and individual case complexity.*
