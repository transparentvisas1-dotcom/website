## HOW PRICING WORKS

### H2
How Our Pricing Works

### Body
Our service fees cover the professional work we do on your behalf - consultation, profile assessment, document preparation, application filing, and follow-up tracking. These are separate from government and embassy fees, which are paid by you directly.

We believe in honest pricing. During your free consultation, we'll provide a complete breakdown of:

1. **Our Service Fee** - The professional fee for handling your visa case end-to-end.
2. **Embassy / Visa Application Fee** - The official fee charged by the destination country's embassy or consulate.
3. **Third-Party Fees** - Any additional costs such as VFS service charges, biometric fees, attestation costs, or translation fees.

No surprises. No hidden charges. No escalating costs after you begin.

## WHAT'S NOT INCLUDED

### H2
Additional Costs to Be Aware Of

### Body
In addition to our service fee, you may need to budget for the following costs, which are paid directly to third parties:

- **Embassy Visa Application Fee** - Set by the destination country (e.g., UK Standard Visitor Visa: £115, Schengen Visa: €80, US B1/B2: $185)
- **VFS / Application Centre Fee** - Service charges at visa application centres
- **Biometric Fee** - Fingerprint and photo fees at some embassies
- **Courier / Delivery Charges** - For passport return and document delivery
- **Medical Examination** - Required for some countries (Australia, Canada, UK for long stays)
- **Police Clearance Certificate** - Required for some visa types

We clearly inform you of all potential additional costs during your initial consultation so you can plan your budget accordingly.
