Visa Application Process

**Q: How do I start my visa application with you?**
A: Simply contact us via phone (+91 88917 44344), WhatsApp, or email (transparentvisas@gmail.com) for a free consultation. We'll discuss your travel plans, assess your eligibility, explain the process, and provide a clear quote — all with no obligation.

**Q: What documents do I need for a visa application?**
A: Document requirements vary by country and visa type, but typically include:
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Passport-size photographs
- Bank statements (last 3–6 months)
- Employment or business proof
- Income Tax Returns (ITR)
- Travel itinerary and accommodation details
- Travel insurance (mandatory for some destinations)
- Purpose-of-visit evidence (invitation letter, admission letter, etc.)
We provide a detailed, country-specific checklist tailored to your case during your consultation.

**Q: How long does the visa process take?**
A: Processing times vary by destination and visa type:
- Tourist/Visit Visas: 1–3 weeks (including our preparation time)
- Student Visas: 5–13 weeks
- Work Visas: 5–13 weeks
- Business Visas: 1–3 weeks
- Refusal Re-applications: 2–4 weeks
These are estimates — we provide a specific timeline for your case during consultation.

**Q: Do you fill out the application form for me?**

**Q: Do I need to visit your office?**

## VISA REFUSAL

### H2
Visa Refusal Questions

**Q: Can you help if my visa was previously refused?**
A: Absolutely. Visa refusal recovery is one of our core specialisations. We analyse your refusal letter, audit your previous application, identify weaknesses, and build a significantly stronger resubmission. We have particular expertise in UK visit visa refusals.

**Q: How soon can I reapply after a refusal?**
A: In most cases, you can reapply immediately. However, we strongly recommend taking adequate time to properly strengthen your application. A rushed resubmission with the same weak documentation will likely result in another refusal. Our typical preparation time for refusal cases is 5–10 working days.

**Q: Will a previous refusal affect my new application?**
A: Yes — visa officers can see your refusal history. This is precisely why the re-application must be substantially stronger than the original. Our strategy specifically addresses previous refusal reasons and demonstrates what has changed since the last application.

**Q: Can you help with multiple refusals?**
A: Yes. We have successfully overturned cases with two and three prior refusals. Multiple refusals require a more strategic approach and stronger documentation, but they are not insurmountable with the right guidance.

## DOCUMENT SERVICES

### H2
Document Service Questions

**Q: What attestation services do you offer?**
A: We handle:
- **HRD Attestation** — State-level Human Resource Department attestation for educational documents
- **MEA Attestation** — Ministry of External Affairs attestation for international use
- **Embassy Attestation** — Embassy-level attestation for specific destination countries
- **WES Attestation** — World Education Services credential evaluation for Canada
- **ICAS Attestation** — International Credential Assessment Service for Australia

**Q: Do you provide document translation?**
A: Yes. We offer certified document translation for personal, educational, and professional documents. Our translations are accepted by embassies and immigration authorities worldwide.

**Q: How long does attestation take?**
A: Attestation timelines vary:
- HRD: 3–7 working days
- MEA: 3–5 working days
- Embassy: 5–15 working days (varies by embassy)
- WES/ICAS: 15–35 working days (includes mailing and processing)
We provide specific estimates for your documents during consultation.
