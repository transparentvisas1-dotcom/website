## UNITED KINGDOM 🇬🇧

### H2
United Kingdom Visa Services

### Body
The UK remains one of the most popular and most complex visa destinations. Whether you need a short visit visa or a long-term work permit, our team has deep expertise in UK immigration requirements and a particularly strong track record with UK visit visa refusal cases.

### Visa Types Available
- **Standard Visitor Visa** - Tourism, family visits, religious conferences, medical treatment (up to 6 months)
- **Student Visa (Student Route)** - Full-time study at a licensed UK institution
- **Skilled Worker Visa** - Employment with an approved UK sponsor
- **Business Visitor Visa** - Conferences, meetings, trade fairs
- **Dependent Visa** - Joining a family member who is in the UK on a valid visa

### Key Requirements
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Completed online application (gov.uk)
- Biometric appointment at VFS centre
- Financial proof (bank statements, ITR, salary slips)
- Travel itinerary and accommodation details
- Supporting documents based on visa type
- TB test certificate (if applicable)

### Processing Time
- Standard: 15 working days
- Priority: 5 working days (where available)
- Super Priority: Next working day (where available)

## UNITED STATES 🇺🇸

### H2
United States Visa Services

### Body
US visa applications require careful preparation, especially for the consular interview. Our team guides you through form DS-160, document preparation, and interview coaching to maximise your chances of approval.

### Visa Types Available
- **B1/B2 Visitor Visa** - Tourism, family visits, medical treatment, business meetings
- **F1 Student Visa** - Academic study at a SEVP-certified institution
- **H1B Work Visa** - Speciality occupation employment (employer-sponsored)
- **L1 Intra-Company Transfer** - Transfer within a multinational company
- **B1 Business Visa** - Conferences, negotiations, trade activities

### Key Requirements
- Valid passport
- DS-160 application confirmation
- Visa fee payment receipt (MRV fee)
- Photograph meeting US specifications
- Financial documentation
- Invitation letter or I-20 (for students)
- Interview appointment at US Consulate

### Processing Time
- Varies by consulate and visa type
- Typically 2–8 weeks after interview
- Premium processing available for certain work visa categories

## CANADA 🇨🇦

### H2
Canada Visa Services

### Body
Canada's immigration system is among the most structured in the world. Whether you are visiting for tourism, pursuing higher education, or exploring work opportunities, we ensure your application meets Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) standards.

### Visa Types Available
- **Temporary Resident Visa (Visitor)** - Tourism, family visits, short-term business
- **Study Permit** - Full-time study at a DLI (Designated Learning Institution)
- **Work Permit** - LMIA-based or LMIA-exempt employment
- **Business Visa** - Trade, conferences, investment exploration

### Key Requirements
- Valid passport
- Completed IRCC online application
- Biometrics (photo and fingerprints)
- Proof of funds and financial stability
- Purpose of visit documentation
- Ties to home country evidence
- Medical examination (if required)
- Police clearance (if required)

### Processing Time
- Visitor Visa: 14–60 days (varies by country of application)
- Study Permit: 4–16 weeks
- Work Permit: 4–12 weeks

## AUSTRALIA 🇦🇺

### H2
Australia Visa Services

### Body
Australia's visa system uses a subclass-based numbering system and requires strong documentation, particularly around financial proof and genuine intent. Our team understands the nuances of Australian immigration, including the Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) requirement for student visas.

### Visa Types Available
- **Visitor Visa (Subclass 600)** - Tourism, family visits, business activities
- **Student Visa (Subclass 500)** - Study at a CRICOS-registered institution
- **Temporary Skill Shortage (Subclass 482)** - Employer-sponsored work visa
- **Business Visa** - Short-term business activities and conferences

### Key Requirements
- Valid passport
- Online application via ImmiAccount
- Health examination (if required)
- Financial proof demonstrating sufficient funds
- GTE statement (for student visas)
- English language proficiency (for student/work visas)
- Health insurance (OSHC for students)

### Processing Time
- Visitor Visa: 15–30 days
- Student Visa: 4–8 weeks
- Work Visa: 4–12 weeks

### H2
Schengen Visa Services (Europe)

### Body
The Schengen visa grants access to 27 European countries with a single application. We help you determine the correct embassy to apply to, prepare your documentation, and navigate the appointment process.

- **Tourist Visa (Type C)** - Tourism and leisure (up to 90 days)
- **Business Visa** - Conferences, meetings, trade fairs

- Valid passport (issued within last 10 years, valid 3+ months beyond stay)

- Two passport-size photographs
- Travel insurance (minimum €30,000 coverage)
- Flight itinerary and accommodation proof
- Financial proof (bank statements for last 3–6 months)
- Employment/business proof
- Cover letter explaining purpose of visit
- Invitation letter (if visiting someone)

### Processing Time
- Standard: 15 calendar days
- Extended: Up to 45 calendar days in exceptional cases

## FAQ SECTION

### H2
Country & Visa FAQ

**Q: How do I know which visa type I need?**
A: Contact us with your destination, purpose of travel, and nationality. We'll assess your situation and recommend the right visa type during a free consultation.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple country visas at the same time?**
A: In most cases, yes. However, some embassies require you to hold a valid passport without ongoing applications. We'll advise you on the best sequencing strategy.

**Q: Do processing times differ based on nationality?**
A: Yes. Some countries process visas faster for certain nationalities. We provide realistic timeline estimates based on your specific nationality and the destination country.

**Q: What if a country I need isn't listed here?**
A: We cover 30+ countries. If your destination isn't listed, contact us - we likely still assist with it or can connect you with the right resources.

**Q: Is travel insurance mandatory for all countries?**
A: It's mandatory for Schengen visas and strongly recommended for all others. Many embassies view travel insurance as a positive signal in your application. We help you get the right policy.
